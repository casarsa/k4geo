Implementation of the **MUon System for Interesting Collision** (MuSIC) with instrumented nozzles
- Compact file: [./MuSIC_v3.1.xml](./MuSIC_v3.1.xml)
- Same as MuSIC_v3, but with nine ForwardTracker silicon disks at |z| = 541, 545, 550, 555, 565,
  575, 580, 585 and 589 cm, each housed in its own 2 cm wide gap cut through the nozzle
  (Nozzle_10deg_v3.xml). The disks at 545, 565 and 585 cm are the three MuSIC_v3 stations.
- The disk positions are the independent numbers of the forward layout: both the nozzle gaps
  and the disk radii are derived from the `NozzleDisk_z*` constants.
- The thinnest piece of nozzle left between two gaps is 2 cm long in z.
